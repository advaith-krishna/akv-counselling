The Journey

How AKV Came to Be

AKV Counselling Centre was founded on the belief that mental wellness deserves the same intentional, holistic care as physical health. After years of working in social welfare and witnessing the profound impact of compassionate listening and evidence-based therapeutic methods, I committed to creating a space where people could truly be heard.

Every session at AKV is guided by a simple principle: your emotional wellbeing matters. Whether you're navigating relationship complexities, building confidence, recovering from trauma, or simply seeking clarity — you deserve a therapist who sees you, respects your pace, and empowers your journey.

Over the past 17 years, I've had the privilege of supporting 500+ individuals toward lasting transformation. Each story, each breakthrough, reinforces my commitment to this meaningful work.

How We Work

Therapeutic Modalities

01

CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)

Helps you identify and transform limiting thought patterns. By understanding the connection between thoughts, feelings, and behaviors, you develop practical strategies for lasting change.

02

Mindfulness & Awareness

Cultivate present-moment awareness to reduce anxiety, build emotional resilience, and connect with inner peace. Techniques grounded in evidence-based mindfulness practices.

03

Systemic Therapy

Understand your challenges within the context of your relationships and environment. Perfect for family dynamics, relational issues, and complex social situations.

04

Humanistic & Person-Centered

Rooted in genuine empathy and unconditional positive regard. You're supported in discovering your own answers, building self-compassion, and actualizing your potential.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Fill out the form above or call +91 99472 62817. We'll confirm your appointment within 24 hours. You can choose in-person or online sessions based on your preference.

Is my information confidential?

Absolutely. All client information is strictly confidential and protected by professional ethics and privacy standards. Your trust is sacred to us.

What if I've never done therapy before?

That's completely okay! Many first-time clients feel nervous, but you'll find a warm, welcoming environment. We'll explain everything and go at your pace.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes! We offer both in-person and online (video call) sessions, depending on your convenience. Both are equally effective and maintained with the same confidentiality.

What is the cancellation policy?

We request 48 hours' notice for cancellations. This respects both your time and our scheduling. Emergency situations are always understood.

How long does therapy take?

This varies based on your needs and goals. Each session is 50-60 minutes. Some clients benefit from short-term support, others prefer ongoing sessions. We discuss this during your first appointment.
